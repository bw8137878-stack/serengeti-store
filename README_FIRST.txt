SERENGETI STORE — LAUNCH STARTER
================================
This is a responsive static website starter. It is not yet a live website and it cannot earn affiliate commissions until you publish it and add valid Amazon Associates tracking IDs issued to you by Amazon.

FILES
- index.html: storefront and product-discovery content
- styles.css: responsive styling
- script.js: marketplace buttons and affiliate-tag setup
- ADS_AND_LAUNCH_GUIDE.txt: ad scripts and launch checklist

FIRST: AMAZON AFFILIATE VS DROPSHIPPING
This website is an Amazon affiliate storefront. Customers click through to Amazon and Amazon handles checkout, fulfilment and returns. You earn commission only on qualifying purchases under Amazon's rules. You cannot add your own markup to Amazon's listing price in this model.

To actually sell under your own price, you would need to operate as the seller, arrange a compliant supplier and fulfilment process, handle returns/customer service, and check all relevant laws/platform rules. Do not buy from Amazon as a workaround to dropship Amazon orders to your customers.

SET TRACKING IDS
Open script.js and replace:
  YOUR_US_ASSOCIATE_TAG
  YOUR_UK_ASSOCIATE_TAG
with the separate tracking IDs Amazon issues for each approved marketplace. Keep the marketplace IDs separate. Do not guess or invent IDs.

PUBLISHING
You need a public HTTPS website URL for the Associates application/review. You can publish these static files using a static hosting service such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Cloudflare Pages. Create an account, upload/deploy the folder, and use the public URL Amazon allows you to list. Review hosting terms and fees before choosing a provider. After publishing, test both buttons on a phone.

IMPORTANT
- Add your actual website URL to the relevant Associates application.
- Apply separately to Amazon.com and Amazon.co.uk if you want to earn from both marketplaces.
- Do not claim to be Amazon or imply Amazon endorses Serengeti Store.
- Do not display a fixed price unless you have a permitted, compliant source and implementation under the applicable Associates policies. This site intentionally tells shoppers to check Amazon for current prices. After approval, update the footer disclosure to the exact required wording: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.”
- Do not claim LED strip lights are the highest-viewed TikTok product; that has not been verified.
- Amazon can change programme rules. Read the current operating agreement and policies before launch.
- If you are under 18, do not misstate your age or bypass eligibility checks. Ask a parent/guardian to review whether they can legitimately own and operate the business/account under the applicable rules and local law.
